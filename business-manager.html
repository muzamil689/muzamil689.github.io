How it works

How is profit calculated?

Revenue is the total of your sales. Cost of goods is what those items cost you when you sold them. Gross profit is revenue minus cost of goods. Net profit or loss is gross profit minus your expenses.

Does it work for any kind of business?

Yes. Shops, online sellers, cafes, workshops and freelancers who sell items can add their products with a cost and a price, record sales and track expenses like rent and salaries.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Use the backup option in Settings so you do not lose it if you clear your browser data.

Does stock update automatically?

Yes. Each sale reduces the stock of that product, and deleting a sale puts the items back.
